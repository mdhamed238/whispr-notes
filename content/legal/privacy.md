# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 12, 2026

WhisprNotes is made by Mohamed Hamed, an independent developer ("I", "me"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the WhisprNotes app.

## The short version

- Your recordings, transcripts, summaries and notes are processed and stored **only on your phone**. They are never uploaded to me or to anyone else.
- There is **no account**, **no analytics**, **no advertising** and **no tracking**.
- The app uses the internet for exactly two things: **downloading AI models** the first time they're needed, and **in-app purchases**. Neither one sends your notes or recordings anywhere.

## What is stored on your phone

- Audio recordings you make.
- Transcripts, titles, tags, summaries and notes you import or write.
- Word timings and waveform data used for playback.
- Your settings (for example haptics, language and model preferences) and the number of AI summaries used this week.
- The AI models the app downloads.

All of this lives in the app's private storage on your device. I have no access to it.

## What uses the internet

**AI model downloads.** The speech-recognition and summary models are downloaded from Hugging Face (huggingface.co) the first time they're needed. Like any download, the request reveals your IP address and standard technical details (such as app and OS version) to Hugging Face. No recordings, notes or personal content are sent. See the [Hugging Face privacy policy](https://huggingface.co/privacy).

**Purchases.** Payments for WhisprNotes Pro are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). The app uses RevenueCat, Inc. to verify purchases and restore them on your devices. RevenueCat receives a random, anonymous app user ID created on your device, your purchase receipts, your device and OS type, and your IP address and country. It does not receive your name, email, recordings or notes. See the [RevenueCat privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy). I can see anonymous purchase records (for example, that a subscription was started) but cannot link them to you.

**Sharing and exporting.** When you share or export a note or recording, it goes only to the app or person you choose, under their own privacy terms.

## What I don't do

- I don't sell, rent or share your information.
- I don't build profiles, track you across apps or websites, or show ads.
- I don't use your content to train AI models.

## Microphone

The app uses the microphone only while you are recording. Audio is turned into text on your phone and saved as a recording you can play back or delete.

## Backups

If your phone's system backup (iCloud or Google backup) is turned on, the app's data may be included in that backup according to your device settings and your backup provider's terms. You can turn this off in your device settings.

## Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can delete individual notes, or erase everything in **Settings → Delete all data**. Uninstalling the app also removes its data from your phone (but not from any device backups you made).

## Children

WhisprNotes is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area), and I do not knowingly collect information from them.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Because your notes and recordings never leave your phone, you control them directly in the app. For purchase records held by Apple, Google or RevenueCat, contact them, or contact me and I'll help.

## Security

Your data is kept in the app's sandboxed storage, protected by your phone's own security and encryption. Purchase traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I'll update the date above and, for significant changes, mention it in the app's release notes.
