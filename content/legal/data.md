# What's stored, and where

Everything you create in WhisprNotes stays on this phone. Here is exactly what the app keeps and what, if anything, ever leaves your device.

| What | Where it's kept | Leaves your phone? |
|---|---|---|
| Audio recordings | This phone | No |
| Transcripts and word timings | This phone | No |
| AI summaries and titles | This phone (made on-device) | No |
| Tags, pins and imported notes | This phone | No |
| Insights and statistics | Calculated on this phone | No |
| Settings | This phone | No |
| AI model downloads | Downloaded from Hugging Face | Your IP address, like any download |
| Purchases | Apple or Google, verified by RevenueCat | Anonymous ID and receipts only |

## No account, no tracking

There is no sign-in, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Nothing you record or write is used to train AI.

## You decide what's shared

A note only leaves your phone when you share or export it, and only to the app or person you pick.

## Delete everything

**Settings → Delete all data** erases every note, recording, summary and export from this phone. Downloaded AI models are kept so you don't have to download them again; you can remove them in **Settings → AI models**.
